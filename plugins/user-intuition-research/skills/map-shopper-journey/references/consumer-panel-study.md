# Consumer panel study policy

Use this shared policy whenever a workflow recruits consumers from the User
Intuition panel by what they buy or do, rather than from the brand's own
customer list. Most packaged-goods brands sell through retailers and have no
list of their shoppers, so the audience comes from a screener.

## 1. Define the audience by behavior

Write the audience as past behavior the participant can answer honestly:

- bought the category in a recent window (for example, the last 30 days for a
  weekly-shop category, the last 6 months for an occasional one);
- how often they buy it;
- where they usually buy it (retailer, channel, online or in store);
- the brand they buy most often, and brands they have tried and stopped buying;
- for rejecters, why the category or brand is not in their basket, asked
  neutrally.

Do not reveal the qualifying answer in the question wording. Ask for a mix of
brands rather than screening only for the user's own brand, unless the study is
about their buyers. Add quota guidance only where the mix matters (for example,
at least a third non-buyers of the brand).

## 2. Check the audience is findable

A narrow audience (a rare brand, one retailer, a niche diet) lowers the incidence
rate. When the user's audience is narrow, say so. If the expected incidence rate
is below 10, the panel workflow submits a feasibility request instead of
launching directly. Each launch fields exactly one country; never infer it.

## 3. Hand off in order

1. `create-study-from-brief` with recruiting method Panel. The brief names the
   decision, the audience in behavioral terms, the markets, and the topics for
   the conversation. The platform builds the plan and the interview; do not
   construct them in the host.
2. `design-screeners` for the behavioral criteria above, if the plan needs them
   sharpened.
3. `field-a-panel` to estimate and launch. Every launch stops for human review of
   the saved plan and the complete quote. Approval of the plan is not approval to
   spend.

Describe the participants as people from a vetted 4M+ consumer panel. Business and
professional audiences are recruited to order per study; never describe them as
part of the panel.

## 4. Evidence rules

Never invent findings, quotes, purchase data, market sizes, or product claims.
Until interviews complete, everything is a plan. Report prevalence as counts
within the sample (X of Y participants), never as market share.
